SECTION 02 // COGNITIVE GROUNDING

BRIDGING HUMAN INTENTION

and continuous physical spatial embodiment.

"Traditional navigation algorithms plan abstract graph routes. Room-Across-Room (RxR) challenges agents to perceive, listen, and physically walk through the continuous visual manifold of human living environments."

Unlike discrete synthetic datasets, RxR features human-generated, visually grounded navigation demonstrations across residential and commercial topologies. Language becomes a dynamic spatial coordinate system anchored to physical landmarks, doorway portals, and real-world floor plans.

Our studio implements an end-to-end embodied AI engine with zero external framework bloat. Trilingual instruction tokens in English, Hindi, and Telugu are parsed through grammatical postpositional models and mapped directly onto 3D topological waypoint graphs.

CROSS-MODAL GROUNDING SPECIFICATION:
• Syntactic SVO / SOV postpositional dependency parsing
• Multi-head cross-attention over Matterport3D visual tokens
• 60 FPS continuous physical walking through architectural doorways
