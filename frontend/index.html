Navigation Workflow

How the Spatial AI Navigates

From natural language command to 3D continuous embodiment in 3 stages.

01

Instruction Encoding

Linguistic tokens in English, Hindi, or Telugu are parsed to detect landmarks (e.g. Sofa, Fridge) and resolve the destination node.

"बैठक कक्ष में सोफा के पास से शुरू करें और रसोईघर में रुकें..."
02

Topological Graph Route

The backend REST engine determines the optimal sequence of viewpoint hops across rooms through open 3D architectural portals.

Path: [vp_1 ➔ vp_2 ➔ vp_3 ➔ vp_4] (Dijkstra)
03

Embodied Trajectory

The agent walks at configurable pace (0.4x - 2.5x), smooths yaw orientation, renders 3D trajectory tubes, and records SPL.

PL: 14.8m • NE: 0.0m • SPL: 1.00 (STOP)
Evaluation & Benchmarks

Multilingual Capabilities & VLN Metrics

Rigorous benchmarking across multilingual natural language instructions and standard embodied agent evaluation criteria.

en-US

English Instruction Parsing

Resolves starting viewpoints, destination rooms, and intermediate traversal constraints across complex spatial prepositions and landmarks.

"Start in Bedroom 1, follow the pathway through the hallway, and reach the Kitchen."
hi-IN

Hindi Postpositional Parsing

Devanagari script tokenization with support for spatial postpositions ("से", "तक", "से होते हुए") and cross-modal attention weight visualization.

"बैठक कक्ष में सोफा के पास से शुरू करें, गलियारे से होते हुए रसोईघर में रुकें।"
te-IN

Telugu Directional Grammar

Agglutinative syntax resolution extracting start markers ("నుండి ప్రారంభించి"), intermediate paths ("గుండా"), and arrival goals ("వెళ్ళండి").

"బెడ్‌రూమ్ 1 నుండి ప్రారంభించి, హాల్‌వే గుండా కిచెన్‌కు వెళ్ళండి."
